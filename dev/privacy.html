1. Application Overview & Scope

stemOS® ("stemOS", "we", "us", or "our") is an educational platform and technical English learning operating system engineered for university engineering students, faculty, and technical professionals across 34 specialized industrial tracks (including semiconductors, aerospace, robotics, automotive engineering, medical devices, and clean energy).

This Privacy Policy explains in detail what information we collect, how we collect it, how we use, store, protect, and share that information, and how you can exercise your rights to access, export, revoke, or delete your data across the https://stemos.org web platform, Progressive Web Application (PWA), RapidCheck CEFR Placement Test, Socratic Feynman AI Tutor, industrial lab simulation crucibles, and verifiable W3C Open Badges 3.0 academic credentialing services.

Public educational visitors may browse our 34-track curriculum catalog, CEFR descriptors, and interactive previews without creating an account. Account registration and Google Sign-In are requested only when a student, instructor, or institutional administrator wishes to save progress across devices, join an institutional classroom cohort, or earn a verified digital certificate.

2. Information We Collect and Access (Data Collection)

stemOS collects and accesses only the specific categories of information necessary to operate our educational platform, authenticate users, track curricular mastery, and issue verifiable academic credentials:

2.1 Google User Data Accessed via Google OAuth 2.0

When you choose to sign in using Google Sign-In (OAuth 2.0 via Google Identity Services and Firebase Authentication), stemOS requests only the basic, non-sensitive identity scopes (openid, profile, and email) to access the following specific Google user data:

Specific Google User Data Categories Accessed:
  • Google Account Unique Identifier (UID / sub): An immutable pseudonymous identifier assigned by Google used solely as the primary key to index and isolate your encrypted learning records in our Cloud Firestore database.
  • Primary Email Address (email): Used to authenticate your account, verify institutional domain enrollment (e.g., @upbc.edu.mx, @tecnm.mx, @uabc.edu.mx), send account/security notifications, and deliver verifiable certificate links.
  • Full Display Name (name): Used to personalize your student or instructor dashboard and to inscribe your verified legal or academic name onto completion diplomas and W3C Open Badges 3.0 digital credentials.
  • Profile Picture URL (picture): Used solely to display your account avatar inside the stemOS navigation bar while you are signed in.

2.2 Educational & Application Data You Provide Directly

  • Account & Institutional Profile Data: If you register via email/password or complete student/instructor onboarding, we collect your full name, email address, role (student, teacher, or institutional administrator), university or educational subsystem (e.g., TecNM, Technological Universities, State Universities), campus name, academic engineering program, selected career specialization track, classroom invite code, and optional student enrollment ID (matrícula).
  • Diagnostic & Learning Progress Data: As you study on stemOS, we collect and store your RapidCheck CEFR Placement Test answers and resulting CEFR level (A1–C1), completed reading and listening stations, vocabulary mastery scores, spaced-repetition review intervals, virtual industrial laboratory crucible results, cumulative study hours, XP/streak metrics, and issued certificate verification folios.
  • Socratic AI Tutor & Pronunciation Practice Inputs: When you interact with the Feynman AI Socratic Tutor or pronunciation drills, we process the text responses you submit to generate real-time pedagogical feedback. Voice pronunciation drills use your browser's native Web Speech API to evaluate phonetic accuracy locally in real time; stemOS does not permanently store raw voice audio recordings on our servers.
  • Institutional & Classroom Inquiries: When university administrators or faculty submit our institutional contact form, we collect the sender's name, institutional email, university name, estimated student cohort size, and message content to respond to the inquiry.

2.3 Automatically Collected Technical & Local Storage Data

  • Browser Local Storage & IndexedDB: We store essential session tokens, language preferences (English/Spanish), offline course caches, and an encrypted/structured offline mutation queue (stemos_sync_queue) on your device so you can continue studying in low-connectivity university laboratories.
  • Security, Network & Performance Logs: Our edge hosting provider (Cloudflare) and authentication provider (Google Firebase) automatically process standard connection metadata—including IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URL, request timestamps, and Core Web Vitals performance telemetry—strictly for DDoS mitigation, bot protection, fraud prevention, and service reliability.
Data Categories stemOS NEVER Requests or Collects:
  • We NEVER request or access Google Drive files, Gmail messages, Google Contacts, Google Calendar events, Google Photos, or device files.
  • We NEVER collect precise real-time GPS geolocation data.
  • We NEVER collect biometric templates, facial recognition data, or permanent voiceprints.
  • We NEVER collect or store raw payment card numbers or bank account credentials on stemOS servers. Optional individual subscriptions are processed directly by PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant processors (Stripe) or interbank SPEI transfer references (see Section 6A).

3. How We Use Your Information (Data Usage)

stemOS uses the Google user data and application data described in Section 2 exclusively to deliver, maintain, and improve user-facing educational functionality:

  • Single Sign-On (SSO) Authentication & Account Management: We use your Google UID, email address, display name, and avatar (or your email/password credentials) to authenticate your identity, maintain your secure session across devices, and associate your learning profile with your university institution.
  • Personalized Curriculum & Cross-Device Progress Synchronization: We use your placement test scores, career track selection, and station completion records to calibrate reading/listening difficulty (CEFR A1 to C1), unlock sequential engineering modules, and synchronize your academic portfolio between desktop and mobile PWA sessions.
  • Verifiable Academic Credential & Badge Issuance: We use your verified full name, institutional affiliation, and module completion records to generate tamper-evident digital diplomas and W3C Open Badges 3.0 credentials signed with cryptographic hashes for academic and employer verification.
  • Instructor Gradebook & Institutional Accreditation Reporting: When a student enrolls in a teacher-managed class using an institutional class code, we display that student's module completion progress, CEFR benchmark level, and diagnostic scores to the authorized instructor and institutional administrator of that specific university campus for academic grading and CACEI/ISO 21001 educational quality audits.
  • Socratic AI Tutoring & Formative Feedback: We use the technical English prompts you enter into the Feynman AI Tutor solely to generate immediate, context-aware pedagogical explanations and rubric evaluations during your active study session.
  • Security, Abuse Prevention & Technical Support: We use connection logs and account identifiers to enforce Cloud Firestore access controls, prevent unauthorized access or automated abuse, debug technical errors, and respond to user support or data rights requests.

Prohibited Uses:

Feynman AI Socratic Tutor Privacy Safeguards

When students engage with our Socratic Feynman AI Tutor to solve engineering case studies (e.g., 8D root-cause analysis, LOTO, SEMI standards), prompts are sent to an AI model (a campus-hosted model when available, otherwise Google's Gemini API) only to generate the tutor's reply. We do not send your Google account email or profile data in prompts, and prompts are not used by us to train AI models. Please do not enter sensitive personal information in the tutor.

6. Data Storage, Security & Protection Mechanisms

We implement rigorous technical, organizational, and physical safeguards to protect all user data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction:

  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmitted between user browsers and stemOS servers is encrypted using modern TLS 1.3 / HTTPS with strict HTTP Strict Transport Security (HSTS) headers.
  • Encryption at Rest: Account and progress records are stored in Google Cloud Firestore (Firebase), which encrypts data at rest by default.
  • Access Controls: Cloud Firestore security rules restrict students to their own authenticated records, and teachers to students enrolled in their own classes.
  • Local Device Storage: Offline course downloads and preferences are stored on your own device (browser localStorage / IndexedDB) and are not sent to us.

6A. Service Providers, Local Storage, Children & Your Rights

Service providers (processors)

We share data only with the providers needed to run stemOS, under their own privacy terms:

  • Google Firebase / Google Cloud — sign-in (Firebase Authentication) and storage of account and progress data (Cloud Firestore).
  • Cloudflare — website hosting, delivery and security; it processes standard connection data such as IP address.
  • Google Gemini API — generates AI tutor replies from the text you type into the tutor.
  • Stripe and SPEI bank transfer — optional paid plans. Stripe collects card details directly; we receive only payment status and an order reference.

We do not sell personal data and do not share Google user data with anyone else, except as required by law or to provide the features described above.

Cookies & local storage

stemOS uses essential browser storage (localStorage, IndexedDB and Firebase Authentication session data) to keep you signed in, remember language and progress, and support offline use. We do not use advertising cookies. Cloudflare may set security cookies. You can clear this storage in your browser at any time.

Children

stemOS is intended for university students and professionals and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data, and object to its processing, by emailing yepzhi@gmail.com (Mexico LFPDPPP ARCO rights). Changes to this policy are posted on this page with an updated date.

stemOS respects each user's absolute ownership over their identity and academic data:

Data Retention Policy:

We retain Google user data only for the length of time needed to fulfill the educational purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy (for example, throughout the student's active enrollment period or university term), or until the user requests deletion. When data is no longer necessary for academic accreditation, it is securely destroyed.

How to Request Immediate Data Deletion:

Any user (student, educator, or institutional administrator) may request the permanent and irreversible deletion of their account and all associated Google user data at any time:

  1. Send an email to our dedicated Privacy Office at yepzhi@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line: "Data Deletion Request — stemOS".
  3. Include your registered Google account email address.

Guarantee: All personal data, OAuth identifiers, and learning records will be completely and permanently purged from our active production databases within seventy-two (72) business hours.

How to Revoke stemOS Google OAuth Permissions:

Users can immediately revoke stemOS's access to their Google Account at any time directly through their Google Account settings:

👉 Visit Google Third-Party Apps & Services Permissions (myaccount.google.com/permissions), select stemOS, and click "Remove Access". Once revoked, stemOS cannot access any Google account information.

Aviso de Privacidad y Gobernanza de Datos

1. Compromiso Institucional y Alcance

En stemOS®, nuestra misión es capacitar a estudiantes de ingeniería, docentes y profesionales técnicos de Universidades Tecnológicas, TecNM y Universidades Estatales en inglés técnico de alta densidad para la industria del Nearshoring.

Reconocemos que la privacidad de la identidad académica, las credenciales institucionales y las interacciones formativas no es un requisito accesorio, sino un principio de diseño arquitectónico. Este Aviso regula el tratamiento de los datos recopilados en la plataforma web stemos.org, la aplicación móvil/PWA, el evaluador socrático Feynman AI Tutor, los crucibles de laboratorio industrial y el sistema de diplomas y credenciales digitales W3C Open Badges 3.0.

2. Principio de Minimización de Datos

Operamos bajo una estricta política de minimización: solo recopilamos la información indispensable para validar la matrícula institucional, asignar la ruta curricular por carrera y certificar los hitos evaluados.

Datos que SÍ recopilamos y procesamos:
  • Identidad y Matrícula Académica: Nombre completo del estudiante, matrícula escolar o número de control, y correo electrónico institucional (ej. @upbc.edu.mx, @tecnm.mx, @uabc.edu.mx).
  • Perfil Curricular: Subsistema universitario (TecNM, UT, Estatal), campus, carrera de adscripción y track de especialización industrial.
  • Progreso Evaluativo: Módulos completados, tiempo de lectura técnica, desempeño en reactivos con interleaving, puntajes en simuladores de laboratorio y folios de diplomas verificables emitidos.
  • Interacciones Socráticas Feynman AI: Respuestas conceptuales de defensa técnica y análisis de causa raíz para evaluación formativa inmediata.
Datos que NUNCA solicitamos ni almacenamos:
  • Geolocalización en tiempo real por GPS.
  • Biometría facial permanente, huellas dactilares o escaneo de iris.
  • Información financiera o números de tarjeta de crédito de alumnos institucionales (las licencias de campus se liquidan mediante factura institucional única sin cobro a los estudiantes).
  • Comunicaciones personales no vinculadas a los módulos técnicos.

3. Divulgación de Datos de Usuario de Google y Requisitos de Uso Limitado

stemOS utiliza Google Sign-In (OAuth 2.0 vía Firebase Authentication) para permitir que estudiantes y profesores se autentiquen de manera segura. Cumplimos rigurosamente con los estándares internacionales de privacidad estudiantil.

El uso y transferencia que stemOS realice a cualquier otra aplicación de la información recibida a través de las APIs de Google se apegará estrictamente a la Política de Datos de Usuario de los Servicios de las APIs de Google, incluidos los requisitos de Uso Limitado.

  • Datos accedidos: UID de Google, dirección de correo electrónico institucional, nombre completo y fotografía de perfil para el avatar de sesión.
  • Finalidad exclusiva: Autenticación de cuenta, sincronización de avance curricular y emisión de certificados digitales verificables con hash SHA-256.
  • Cero venta o transferencia publicitaria: Los datos de Google nunca se venden ni se transfieren a intermediarios de datos, anunciantes o redes publicitarias.
  • Cero entrenamiento de IA general: Los datos de los usuarios de Google nunca se utilizan para desarrollar o entrenar modelos públicos de Inteligencia Artificial ni aprendizaje automático.

4. Ejercicio de Derechos ARCO y Supresión de Datos

De acuerdo con la Ley Federal de Protección de Datos Personales en Posesión de los Particulares (LFPDPPP), todo estudiante, docente o titular de datos tiene derecho a ejercer en cualquier momento sus derechos de Acceso, Rectificación, Cancelación y Oposición (ARCO):

  • Acceso: Solicitar copia íntegra de su historial académico y avance curricular registrado.
  • Rectificación: Corregir su nombre, matrícula o carrera en caso de error en los certificados.
  • Cancelación y Supresión Definitiva: Solicitar la eliminación total de su cuenta y registros de progreso en un plazo máximo de 72 horas hábiles mediante correo a yepzhi@gmail.com.
  • Revocación de Permisos Google: Los usuarios pueden revocar el acceso de stemOS a su cuenta de Google en cualquier momento en myaccount.google.com/permissions.
